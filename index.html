When to Use This Template

Use this onboarding request template only when a service provider is sourced directly by a Care Partner (SAH) or Service Manager (NDIS), rather than through the Recruitment team.

A service provider is any individual or organisation engaged to deliver services that is not directly employed — including independent contractors, sole traders, and subcontracted workers.

Implementation

1. Self-Managed Services (SAH only – not applicable to NDIS)

  • Care Partners must ensure that services MUST NOT commence until onboarding is fully completed.
  • The compliance team will complete the onboarding and notify the relevant Care Partner once onboarding is finalised.
  • Services may only commence after confirmation from the compliance team.

2. Provider Managed Services (Applicable to SAH & NDIS)

  • Care Partners/Case Managers should prioritise using existing approved resources wherever possible before sourcing new ones.
  • All new resources must be approved prior to onboarding:
    • Home Care (Melbourne & Sydney): approval required from Aneta
    • NDIS: approval required from Harry
  • If a new resource is identified: proposed pricing must not exceed 80% of standard rates, using the current home care price list. For NDIS, use the pricing guide. Exceptions can be made but must be approved by Aneta or Harry.
  • Care Partners/Case Managers must confirm approval directly with the relevant approver before submitting any onboarding request.
  • Once approval is obtained, proceed to creating a task on Visual Care.
  • The Compliance team will complete onboarding and notify the relevant Care Partner/Case Manager once finalised. Services MUST NOT commence until onboarding is fully completed.

Holistic Assessment Due

CONSUMER NAME:
BUDGET CHECKED: Yes / No
CONSUMER RESPONSE: Accepted / Declined
REASON IF DECLINED:
ASSIGN TO: RN Team (if accepted) or Compliance Team (if declined)
  • Select the category SAH 120 – Holistic Assessment Due.
  • Holistic assessments are due every 6 months. The task is assigned to the Care Partner when due. Care Partners can also raise the first one themselves.
  • The Compliance Team will allocate these tasks to Care Partners 1 month before expiry.
  • If the client declines and there are significant risks where not completing an assessment could cause issues, a Dignity of Risk form must be signed.
  1. Care Partner: check in with the client, check the budget, and speak to the client to line up a holistic assessment as part of our routine review or initial onboarding. If the client declines, record the reason. Assign the task to the RN Team if accepted, or the Compliance Team if declined.
  2. RN Team: complete the assessment, record the date of assessment in the comments, and either update the existing files or upload new ones to OneDrive. If new files replace old ones, move the old files into the Archive subfolder within the same assessments folder, Tab 3 – Nursing (create one if it does not exist). Re-assign the task to the Compliance Team.
  3. Compliance Team: if the assessment was done, check the documentation is complete, files are updated or new files are in place with old files archived, and set the next due date 6 months from the date of assessment. If the client declined, set the next due date 6 months from the date the task was received. Close the task.

ISO CODE (AT/HM only, leave blank if not AT-HM):

JUSTIFICATION (if no prescription): (the assessed need this item addresses, e.g. from the support plan or care plan)

CLIENT AWARE OF CO-CONTRIBUTION + OUR FEES: Yes / No (estimated $)
DELIVERY OR INSTALL: (address if not home, contact, access notes, preferred days/times)
ASSIGN TO: Ordering Team (non-AT-HM items) or Operations Manager (AT-HM items)
  • Select the category SAH 30 – Purchasing/Ordering.
  • Please check the client’s budget before submitting to ensure they have funds for the purchase.
  • Quote only: skip price, consent and co-contribution. Once the client agrees, record the exact price, co-contribution and consent in the comments section of the task, then:
    • AT-HM items: assign the task to the Operations Manager for approval.
    • Non-AT-HM items: assign the task straight to the Ordering Team. No Operations Manager approval needed.
  • Order: the client has agreed to the item and the price. Nothing is ordered without CLIENT CONSENT marked Yes.
  • If the quote is to support an AT-HM funding application, skip the ISO code and prescription. Operations Manager approval is not needed.
  • All AT-HM orders must be assigned to and approved by the Operations Manager before the ordering team places them. Non-AT-HM orders do not need Operations Manager approval.
  • AT-HM funding only covers items on the AT-HM list. Anything else is General and comes from the quarterly budget or is private.
  • Attach quotes and prescriptions to the task. If the file is too big, upload it to OneDrive and attach the link instead.

Equipment Trial Request

ITEM(S): (one line per item: name, supplier link or website, quantity, supplier contact details if applicable)
URGENT: Yes / No (if Yes, needed by when)
ASSIGN TO: Ordering Team
  • This request is for internal staff only. In Sydney we do not have internal allied health staff, so this process does not apply there.
  • Select the category SAH 30 – Equipment Trial.
  • Workflow (in order of events):
  1. Ordering Team: once you receive the task, contact the supplier to obtain the purchase quote for the item, the trial fee (if applicable), and confirm whether the trial can be done at the client’s home or only at the showroom. If showroom, write the address and ensure it is the closest to the client’s house. Record all of this in the comments section of the task, attach the quote, and reassign the task back to the Care Partner.
  2. Care Partner: gain client consent for the trial, quoting our cost plus any additional charges from the supplier if any. Record the exact price, co-contribution and consent in the comments section of the task, then assign the task to the Rostering Team.
  3. Rostering Team: contact the supplier to lock in the date and time and inform the client. Cap the trial at 2 hours where possible. Add a roster note on the shift stating where the trial will take place (client’s home or showroom, with address), and ensure the correct shift type for equipment trial is selected.
  4. Care Partner: once the trial goes well and the client wants to proceed, create an Ordering Request task to purchase the item.
