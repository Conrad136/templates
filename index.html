When to Use This Template

Use this onboarding request template only when a service provider is sourced directly by a Care Partner (SAH) or Service Manager (NDIS), rather than through the Recruitment team.

A service provider is any individual or organisation engaged to deliver services that is not directly employed — including independent contractors, sole traders, and subcontracted workers.

Implementation

1. Self-Managed Services (SAH only – not applicable to NDIS)

  • Care Partners must ensure that services MUST NOT commence until onboarding is fully completed.
  • The compliance team will complete the onboarding and notify the relevant Care Partner once onboarding is finalised.
  • Services may only commence after confirmation from the compliance team.

2. Provider Managed Services (Applicable to SAH & NDIS)

  • Care Partners/Case Managers should prioritise using existing approved resources wherever possible before sourcing new ones.
  • All new resources must be approved prior to onboarding:
    • Home Care (Melbourne & Sydney): approval required from Aneta
    • NDIS: approval required from Harry
  • If a new resource is identified: proposed pricing must not exceed 80% of standard rates, using the current home care price list. For NDIS, use the pricing guide. Exceptions can be made but must be approved by Aneta or Harry.
  • Care Partners/Case Managers must confirm approval directly with the relevant approver before submitting any onboarding request.
  • Once approval is obtained, proceed to creating a task on Visual Care.
  • The Compliance team will complete onboarding and notify the relevant Care Partner/Case Manager once finalised. Services MUST NOT commence until onboarding is fully completed.

Holistic Assessment Due

CONSUMER NAME:
BUDGET CHECKED: Yes / No
CONSUMER RESPONSE: Accepted / Declined
REASON IF DECLINED:
ASSIGN TO: Rostering Team (if accepted) or Compliance Team (if declined)
  • Select the category SAH 120 – Holistic Assessment Due.
  • Holistic assessments are due every 6 months. The task is assigned to the Care Partner when due. Care Partners can also raise the first one themselves.
  • The Compliance Team will allocate these tasks to Care Partners 1 month before expiry.
  • If the client declines and there are significant risks where not completing an assessment could cause issues, a Dignity of Risk form must be signed.
  1. Care Partner: check in with the client, check the budget, and speak to the client to line up a holistic assessment as part of our routine review or initial onboarding. If the client declines, record the reason. Assign the task to the Rostering Team if accepted, or the Compliance Team if declined.
  2. Rostering Team: liaise with the RN Team to lock in the visit and confirm the date and time with the client. Roster the visit under Nursing Comprehensive Assessment. Once booked, re-assign the task to the RN Team.
  3. RN Team: complete the assessment, record the date of assessment in the comments, and either update the existing files or upload new ones to OneDrive. If new files replace old ones, move the old files into the Archive subfolder within the same assessments folder, Tab 3 – Nursing (create one if it does not exist). Re-assign the task to the Compliance Team.
  4. Compliance Team: if the assessment was done, check the documentation is complete, files are updated or new files are in place with old files archived, and set the next due date 6 months from the date of assessment. If the client declined, set the next due date 6 months from the date the task was received. Close the task.

  • If we are charging the client when a ‘leave’ is applied to a shift, the system only allows us to set the cancellation charge percentage. This can only be set as 0 or 1. We do not use partial cancellation charges (e.g. 80% as 0.8).

This workflow is for the Recruitment Team once they receive this task.

Rostering requests (internal support workers)

  • Recruit and onboard the support worker (the Employees steps below apply), then assign the task back to the Rostering Team once done.

Quote required

  • Get the quote and provide it to whoever raised the task (usually the Service Coordination Team).
  • They will update you if the provider is to be onboarded.

Employees

  • Onboard if the pay is within the negotiation levels on the pay matrix.
  • If the candidate asks for more, escalate to Conrad.

Service providers

  • Onboard if the rate is no more than 80% of our standard rates:
    • SAH (EnableU and Esse Care): OneDrive > ENABLEU > Service SAH > Home Care Templates (EnableU) > SAH Intake Folder > SAH EnableU Service Pricing Schedule (use the latest version). Esse Care uses the same price list.
    • NDIS: NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits at ndis.gov.au/providers/pricing-and-payments/pricing/pricing-arrangements.
  • If the rate is above 80% of the price list, get approval from Maria (SAH) or Harry (NDIS) before onboarding.

Once onboarded

  • Reassign the task back to whoever raised it (e.g. Service Coordination or Rostering) with a comment confirming onboarding is completed, and include:
    • the name of the employee or service provider
    • the rate (not needed for Rostering requests)
    • any other relevant details (e.g. contact details, availability, start date)
